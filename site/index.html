Discord Wrap Bot

A self-hosted bot that keeps activity stats for your server and turns them into a yearly wrap: image slides of who talked most, the most reacted messages, time in voice and the longest call.

Install it Source on GitHub

Nobody is tracked without saying yes

  • Members opt in themselves.Nothing about a member is recorded until they run /optin. Admins can't opt anyone in, by name or by role.
  • Admins only choose where.Even opted-in members are only counted in channels an admin whitelisted.
  • Message text is never read.The bot stores who sent a message, where and when. It doesn't have permission to read what it says.
  • Leaving deletes your data./optout stops tracking and deletes everything stored about you. Removing the bot from a server deletes that server's data.
  • Your data stays with whoever hosts it.Each server owner runs their own copy of the bot with its own database. Nothing is sent to a central service.

Commands for everyone

/optin

Lets the bot count your messages, reactions and time in voice in this server. The reply lists the channels where you're counted.

/optout

Stops tracking you and deletes your messages, reactions and voice time in this server, after you confirm. Deleted history can't be brought back, even if you opt in again.

Reactions other people left on your messages stay counted for them, but no longer count as received by you.

/stats

Server leaderboards: top senders, busiest channels, top reactors, who gets reacted to most, top emoji, the most reacted message, most time in voice, the longest call and the busiest voice moment. Name a member to see their own numbers instead.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
usernoneShow one member's messages, reactions given and received, favourite emoji, most active channel and voice time.
periodAll timeLast 7 days, Last 30 days, This year or All time.
publicfalseTrue posts the result in the channel. Otherwise only you see it.

/wrap server · /wrap me · /wrap member

The year as a deck of image slides you click through privately with Back and Next. Share this slide posts the current slide in the channel, with a button others can use to open the full wrap.

OptionDefaultWhat it does
serverThe whole server's year: totals, the regulars, when it was busiest, the hall of fame and voice.
meYour own year: your numbers and rank, your biggest fan, your favourite emoji, your longest call and call buddy.
member userSomeone else's year, if they opted in.
previewadminsfalseSee this year so far, any time of year. Only you see it, and it can't be shared.

The wrap opens during wrap week, December 25 to 31 by default. The rest of the year, /wrap shows last year's wrap and a countdown to the next one.

Commands for admins

These need the Manage Server permission. Server owners can change who may use each command in Server Settings → Integrations.

/whitelist

Chooses the channels where opted-in members are counted. Whitelisting a category covers every channel and thread in it, and whitelisting a voice channel covers its text chat too.

SubcommandOptionWhat it does
addchannelStart counting activity in this channel or category.
removechannelStop counting it. Data already stored stays; use /purge-data to delete it.
listThe whitelisted channels and how many members opted in. It doesn't say who.

/tracking

SubcommandOptionWhat it does
pauseStop recording anything new in the whole server. Stored stats stay.
resumeStart recording again.
statusWhether tracking is on, how many channels are whitelisted and how many members opted in.

/purge-data

Deletes stored stats, after a confirmation. It doesn't change who is tracked from now on.

SubcommandOptionWhat it does
useruserDelete everything stored for one member. If they're opted in, new activity keeps being counted.
channelchannelDelete everything from one channel or thread. A channel's threads are purged separately.

/wrap-channel

Posts the first slide of the server wrap once, when wrap week opens.

SubcommandOptionWhat it does
setchannelPost the wrap in this text or announcement channel. The bot checks that it can post and attach images there first.
clearDon't post the wrap automatically.
showShow the current wrap channel.

/silent

OptionDefaultWhat it does
enabledrequiredTrue mutes the bot's announcements, including the wrap post. False turns them back on.

What's stored

Messages
Message ID, channel, author and time. Never the text.
Reactions
Who reacted, with which emoji, on which message, and whose message it was.
Voice
Who was in which voice channel, from when to when. The AFK channel isn't counted.
Settings
The channel whitelist, who opted in, and whether tracking is paused.

Deleting a message or removing a reaction deletes it from the database too. Bots are never tracked.
